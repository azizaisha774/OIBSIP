# Task 6: Research Report - The Importance of Patch Management

A research report explaining what patch management is, why unpatched systems are one of the largest attack surfaces in cybersecurity, and how organizations can build an effective patching strategy.

## Report

[**patch\_management\_report.md**](./patch_management_report.md)

## What the report covers

|Section|Content|
|-|-|
|1. Introduction|Definition of patch management and its role in the vulnerability lifecycle|
|2. Why Patches Matter|How vulnerabilities are discovered, reported (CVE), scored (CVSS) and exploited; case studies of **WannaCry/EternalBlue** and the **Equifax** breach|
|3. Consequences of Not Patching|Data breaches, ransomware, compliance violations, financial penalties, with statistics (Verizon DBIR 2025, IBM Cost of a Data Breach)|
|4. Patch Management Lifecycle|Discovery → Assessment → Testing → Deployment → Verification|
|5. Best Practices|Prioritized 7-step patch management checklist|
|6. Challenges|Legacy systems, downtime, testing requirements, and how to overcome each|
|7. Conclusion|Summary of key points|
|8. References|12 sources including NIST SP 800-40 Rev. 4, CISA KEV, CVE/NVD, Microsoft MS17-010, GAO, FTC|
