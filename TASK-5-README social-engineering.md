## Objective

Research and document how social engineering attacks work, why they are among the most effective attack vectors, and how individuals and organisations can defend against them.

## Key takeaways

* Social engineering targets **people, not technology**, so it can bypass firewalls and antivirus.
* Attacks rely on a few repeating psychological levers: **urgency, authority, trust, curiosity and the wish to help**.
* Even security vendors and military networks have been breached through one email or one USB drive.
* Strong defence combines **phishing-resistant MFA, email and device controls, identity verification procedures, and regular awareness training**.
